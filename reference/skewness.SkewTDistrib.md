# Skewness of the Skew t Distribution

Closed form for \\\nu \> 3\\, from Azzalini and Capitanio (2003); `NaN`
otherwise. Unlike the skew normal's, it is unbounded.

## Arguments

- x:

  A
  [`SkewTDistrib`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/SkewTDistrib.md).

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
