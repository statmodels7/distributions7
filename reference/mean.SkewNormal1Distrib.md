# Mean of the Skew Normal Distribution

Closed form: \\\mu + \sigma b \delta\\ with \\\delta =
\alpha/\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}\\ and \\b = \sqrt{2/\pi}\\.

## Arguments

- x:

  A
  [`SkewNormal1Distrib`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/SkewNormal1Distrib.md).

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
