# Beta Fourth-Order Derivatives in Its Shapes

Closed form and free of the data, so `expected` changes nothing.

## Arguments

- distrib:

  A `Beta2Distrib` object.

- y:

  A numeric vector of observations.

- theta:

  A list with `alpha` and `beta`.

- expected:

  Logical; makes no difference here.

- scale:

  Either `"parameter"` or `"link"`; handled by the generic.

- approx:

  Ignored.

- nsim:

  Ignored.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of fourth-derivative components.
