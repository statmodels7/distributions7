# Kurtosis of the Weibull Distribution

Closed form, excess: \\(g_4 - 4g_1g_3 + 6g_1^2g_2 - 3g_1^4)/(g_2 -
g_1^2)^2 - 3\\, free of the scale.

## Arguments

- x:

  A
  [`Weibull1Distrib`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/Weibull1Distrib.md).

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
