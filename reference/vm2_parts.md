# The Pieces a von Mises Derivative in rho Needs

The concentration, its four derivatives in \\\rho\\, and the derivatives
of \\A\\ at that concentration, computed once per call.

## Usage

``` r
vm2_parts(theta)
```

## Arguments

- theta:

  A list with `mu` and `rho`.

## Value

A list with `kappa`, `kd` and `ad`.
