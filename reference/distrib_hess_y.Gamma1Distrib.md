# Gamma Second Response Derivative in Mean and Dispersion

\\\partial^2\ell/\partial y^2 = -(a-1)/y^2\\.

## Arguments

- distrib:

  A `Gamma1Distrib` object.

- y:

  A numeric vector of observations.

- theta:

  A list with `mu` and `phi`.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
