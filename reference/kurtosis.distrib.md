# Kurtosis of a Distribution

Computes the excess kurtosis, the standardized fourth central moment
minus three through
[`moment`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/moment.md).

## Arguments

- x:

  A `distrib` object.

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Passed to
  [`moment`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/moment.md).

## Value

A numeric vector.
