# Kurtosis of the Generalised Pareto Distribution

Closed form: excess \\3(1-2\xi)(2\xi^2+\xi+3)/\\(1-3\xi)(1-4\xi)\\ - 3\\
for \\\xi \< 1/4\\, and infinite at or above one quarter.

## Arguments

- x:

  A `GPDDistrib`.

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
