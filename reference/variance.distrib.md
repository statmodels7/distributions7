# Variance of a Distribution

Computes the second central moment through
[`moment`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/moment.md),
the mean being evaluated first and passed as the centre.

## Arguments

- x:

  A `distrib` object.

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Passed to
  [`moment`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/moment.md).

## Value

A numeric vector.
