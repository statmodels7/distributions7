# Moments of the von Mises in the Resultant Length

The ordinary moments of \\Y\\ as a number on \\\[-\pi, \pi)\\, obtained
numerically as they are for
[`vonmises1_distrib`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/vonmises1_distrib.md):
\\\mu\\ is the mean *direction* and \\\rho\\ the mean resultant length,
and neither is an ordinary moment.

## Arguments

- x:

  A `VonMises2Distrib` object.

- theta:

  A list with `mu` and `rho`.

- ...:

  Passed on.

## Value

A numeric vector.
