# Skewness of the Skew Normal Distribution

Closed form: \\\tfrac{4-\pi}{2}(b\delta)^3/(1 - b^2\delta^2)^{3/2}\\.
Its range is \\(-0.9953, 0.9953)\\, whatever the shape.

## Arguments

- x:

  A
  [`SkewNormalDistrib`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/SkewNormalDistrib.md).

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
