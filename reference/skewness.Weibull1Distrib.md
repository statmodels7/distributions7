# Skewness of the Weibull Distribution

Closed form: \\(g_3 - 3g_1g_2 + 2g_1^3)/(g_2 - g_1^2)^{3/2}\\, free of
the scale.

## Arguments

- x:

  A
  [`Weibull1Distrib`](https://statmodels7.github.io/distributions7/reference/Weibull1Distrib.md).

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
