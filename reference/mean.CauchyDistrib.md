# The Cauchy Distribution Has No Moments

`NaN`. No moment of the Cauchy exists, the tails being too heavy for
\\\int \|y\| f(y) \\ dy\\ to converge, and that is the honest answer
rather than the divergent quadrature the numerical default would
attempt.

## Arguments

- x:

  A `CauchyDistrib`.

- theta:

  A named list of parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector of `NaN`.
