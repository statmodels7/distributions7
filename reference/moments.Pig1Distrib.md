# Closed Moments of the Poisson-Inverse Gaussian

The cumulants of the mixture are those of the Poisson plus those the
inverse Gaussian rate contributes: \\\kappa_1 = \mu\\, \\\kappa_2 =
\mu + \sigma\mu^2\\, \\\kappa_3 = \mu + 3\sigma\mu^2 + 3\sigma^2\mu^3\\,
\\\kappa_4 = \mu + 7\sigma\mu^2 + 18\sigma^2\mu^3 + 15\sigma^3\mu^4\\;
skewness and excess kurtosis are the standardized ratios.

## Arguments

- x:

  A `Pig1Distrib` object.

- theta:

  A list containing `mu` and `sigma`.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector.
